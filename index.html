About

I'm a student with a growing interest in economics and the way it explains, and sometimes fails to explain, the choices people make. Mathematics is part of what draws me in: I like questions that can be reasoned about carefully and tested against evidence.

I'm especially curious about public policy and behavioural economics, and about how ideas from theory hold up when they meet real communities and real constraints. I use this site to think through those questions in writing, and to share what I'm reading and learning as I go.
